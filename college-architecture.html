Technical Bottlenecks & Considerations

High AI Cost Management
Claude API calls are expensive at scale. Unlimited chat on the Pro plan means unpredictable costs. A single heavy user could cost more than their subscription.
Solution: Implement token budgets per user per day. Cache common responses. Use smaller models for simple queries, Claude for complex counseling. Monitor cost-per-user closely and adjust tier limits.
High FERPA Compliance (Student Data)
Student education records are protected under FERPA. If schools or districts use the platform, you must comply with strict data handling requirements. Violations risk federal funding for partner schools.
Solution: Encrypt all student data at rest and in transit. Implement strict access controls. Sign data processing agreements with school partners. Never share student data with third parties. Maintain audit logs.
High AI Safety & Accuracy
AI giving wrong admissions advice (incorrect deadlines, bad financial estimates, hallucinated requirements) could harm students. Liability and trust are at stake.
Solution: Ground AI responses in verified college data (IPEDS/Scorecard). Add disclaimers. Implement human review for critical advice. Flag low-confidence responses. Maintain a curated knowledge base.
High PCI Compliance for Payments
Handling payment information requires PCI DSS compliance. Storing or transmitting card data incorrectly means liability for breaches and fines.
Solution: Use Stripe Elements or Stripe Checkout exclusively. Card data never touches your servers — Stripe handles it all. You just receive tokens and webhook events.
Medium Multi-Tenant Data Isolation
Schools, families, and individual students on one platform. A bug could expose one student's essays or grades to another user. Critical for trust and compliance.
Solution: Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforced at the database level. Every query filtered by user/org. Parent accounts scoped to their children only. Test exhaustively.
Medium College Data Freshness
College requirements, deadlines, tuition, and acceptance rates change yearly. Stale data leads to bad recommendations and erodes trust.
Solution: Automated pipeline to sync IPEDS/Scorecard data annually. Manual verification for top 200 schools. Timestamp all data and surface "last updated" to users. Community reporting for errors.
Medium Essay Plagiarism & Academic Integrity
If the AI writes essays (rather than guides), colleges may flag or reject applications. The platform could develop a reputation that hurts users.
Solution: AI must guide, never write. Implement guardrails that refuse to generate full essays. Track revision history to show student authorship. Educate users on academic integrity in onboarding.
Low Email Deliverability
Deadline reminders and progress emails to students/parents can trigger spam filters. Gmail and Yahoo have strict requirements for bulk senders.
Solution: Use Resend or SendGrid with proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records. Warm up sending reputation gradually. Include easy unsubscribe. Segment by engagement.
